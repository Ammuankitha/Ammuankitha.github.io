03 / M.TECH RESEARCH

Conjugate Heat Transfer in a Circular Nozzle

IIT Kanpur · Aug 2024 – 2025

Numerically investigated conjugate heat transfer in a circular nozzle with ejector-assisted passive cooling using ANSYS Fluent. The study examined coupled flow and heat-transfer behaviour to evaluate nozzle-wall thermal protection.

  • Modelled coupled fluid flow, wall conduction, and convection.
  • Analysed temperature distribution, heat flux, thermal gradients, and cooling effectiveness.
  • Used the SST k–ω turbulence model to capture near-wall flow and thermal gradients.
